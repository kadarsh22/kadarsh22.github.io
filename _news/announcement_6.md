🚀 Our new work **["Self-Supervised Collapse Prevention with Score-Based Entropy Maximization"](https://kadarsh22.github.io/score_ent.github.io/)** is now available on **[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11379)**! We introduce Entropy-Based Regularization (EBReg), which uses a learned latent score model to prevent representation collapse in self-supervised learning. Explore the **[project page](https://kadarsh22.github.io/score_ent.github.io/)** and **[alphaXiv discussion](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2611.11379)**.

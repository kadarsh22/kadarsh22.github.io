👋 Hi, I'm Adarsh Kappiyath, a PhD student at the University of Surrey, working under the supervision of **[Dr. Lu Yin](https://luuyin.com/)** and **[Dr. Xiatian Zhu](https://surrey-uplab.github.io/)**.  

🔬 My research focuses on self-supervised learning and out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization. I primarily work on improving non-generative architectures such as JEPA, enhancing their ability to learn structured world models and generalize beyond the training distribution. 

🚀 I'm actively seeking **internships and research collaborations**! If you're interested in working together, feel free to reach out.
